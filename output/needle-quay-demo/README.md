# Needle Quay: interactive groovebox revision

Unzip the complete package, run `Launch-NeedleQuay.ps1`, then press Play in Roblox Studio. It installs checksum-verified companion content locally. Nothing is uploaded or published. Rerun the launcher after a Studio update.

Walk to an activity and press E, use X on a controller, or click the nearby action button. Building entrances now offer a working route to their host. Naya's record activity has a Listen button and three musical-role choices. The journal shows readable objectives and progress.

Enter your Roamer and open Sounds. Use the pad arrows to choose a pad, then audition and edit its real waveform. Tone provides level, pitch/speed and trim. ADSR provides attack, decay, sustain, release and gate, with an envelope overlay. FX provides echo time/send and room reverb, plus Dry, Dotted echo and Small room presets. Reset sound edits restores the selected pad's controls. It preserves its sample and notes.

Sound edits belong to each pad and pattern. Beat Shelf snapshots include them. Pitch changes playback speed; this is not independent time stretching. Saves in this unpublished Studio build last for the current Play session only.

This package continues the existing local art revision. It is not final artbook acceptance. Full multiplayer, device performance, gamepad/touch acceptance and perceptual audio review remain open. Source notes and Studio evidence are in `docs/needle-quay/GROOVEBOX_2026-10-25.md` and `docs/evidence/needle-quay-groovebox` in the repository.
