# Needle Quay — four separate views per building

52 PNG files for all 13 buildings in the approved artbook. Each building has its own folder containing exactly four image files:

- front.png — camera from -Z, main entrance elevation
- left.png — camera from -X; front end at image right
- right.png — camera from +X; front end at image left
- back.png — camera from +Z, rear elevation

Open index.html to browse the set on checkerboard, white or dark backgrounds. The original PNGs preserve genuine alpha; preview-only RGB matte colors in fully transparent pixels do not constitute an opaque background. All outputs are below 20 MB. Individual provenance JSON files retain exact prompts, references and original generated-image paths. manifest.json records dimensions, bytes, alpha extrema and SHA-256 hashes.

These are newly derived, detailed concept elevations matching the artbook designs, not exact camera renders of one reconstructed DCC asset. Small geometry, framing and lighting differences can remain between views. Rear and unseen wall details are interpretations. Historical artbook approval does not automatically approve these new references. No Tripo generation, upload or Roblox publication was performed. Canonical asset dimensions/contracts and original approved sheets were not modified.
