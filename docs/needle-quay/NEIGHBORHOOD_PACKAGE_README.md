# Needle Quay / neighborhood demo

Extract the full ZIP and run Launch-NeedleQuay.ps1. It installs only the checksum-verified needle-quay content namespace into local Studio. Press Play. The place is unpublished and saves last for this Play session.

A small objective card shows the next main activity. Guide ON marks its destination; select any unfinished activity in the journal to track that instead. Guidance is optional, gives no rewards and does not teleport. Your own Roamer is the destination when music needs making.

Meet Tori, Ivo, Naya, Sana, Mica and Ronan for activities. Ask a question opens three optional conversation topics. Warm Current Cafe now offers a conversation with Nell; no extra job or sample collection is required there. Harbor Market Hall leads to Sana; Listen with Naya takes you to the record activity.

Completed activities gradually warm their own corner. Community boards show your personal progress. Residents greet nearby players and move between short walking and pause segments. Effects OFF freezes decorative movement while text, task guidance and progress remain available.

The prior companion notebook, saved-beat observations, sampler FX/ADSR/sample editing, A-D patterns and six-slot shelf are retained. No new sound bank, mandatory quest, upload or paid service is added.

Art remains candidate/in review. Focused desktop Studio observations do not replace physical-device, independent multiplayer, cloud-save or complete-route acceptance. See docs/needle-quay/NEIGHBORHOOD_PASS_2026-10-26.md in the repository.
