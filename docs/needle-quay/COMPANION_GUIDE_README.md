# Needle Quay - Companion Mission Guide

Extract the whole ZIP, double-click Launch-NeedleQuay.cmd and press Play in Studio. All previous missions, street activities, voiced reward reactions, wardrobe and discoverable FX are included. Everything remains local; no Roblox uploads or publishing.

## Companion mission voices
Choose Alissia or Nikolas. Each has 20 new ElevenLabs lines covering cases, repair controls, record matching, metal/chirp steps, A/B variation, shelf saving, returning to hosts, the finale, four FX discoveries and the FX walkthrough. The established Jessica/Robert casting is retained.

Automatic hints wait eight seconds after a mission step changes and speak once for that step. The mission card's ? button requests the current voice hint. Identical lines have a 30-second cooldown. Companion Hints OFF suppresses instructions; choosing Alone suppresses all companion speech. Voice volume, mute and subtitle settings apply. Conversations, street games, recording, playback, listening and sample auditions take priority. Workshop-specific instructions can play inside the Roamer while transport is idle.

New FX discoveries queue their named voice reaction and a companion cheer after the immediate completion feedback. Queues expire after 90 seconds and do not replay on loading existing progress. Cosmetic reward chatter waits for companion speech.

## Learn an FX on BeatMakka
Go to Roamer > Sounds > Discovered FX and press SHOW ME. It chooses an earned recipe not yet taught during this session.
1. Select the pad with the existing pad arrows. Press USE THIS PAD on the waveform (or confirm in the tutorial strip).
2. The chosen recipe's APPLY button glows amber. Press it yourself. The walkthrough waits until the server-returned pad settings match the recipe.
3. The view returns to the sample controls and highlights AUDITION. Press it to hear your pad. Companion speech pauses for the preview and its FX tail.
4. After the preview, the companion reminds you to save your beat. The tutorial does not claim a save or award progress automatically.

The cross cancels the walkthrough. Leaving Sounds or the studio cancels it; changing pads returns it to pad selection. Visual guidance works with no companion selected. Recipes remain available in the regular manual controls. No tutorial action silently changes or saves music.

## Production and acceptance
40 new runtime WAV clips, approximately 1.5-5.2 seconds each, were cut from two sets of four generated takes. All eight full masters, generation IDs, selected take boundaries and SHA-256 provenance remain in assets/production/needle-quay/guide-voices. Cuts use measured phrase pauses; audio is mono PCM16 at 44.1kHz with -19 LUFS / -2dB TP normalization targets. ElevenLabs reported 10,136 speech credits, estimated $2.0272. No regeneration was submitted.

Source formatted and local package built. No tests were added or run. Studio listening, mobile layout, mission-flow and multiplayer acceptance remain unverified. Saves remain session-only in Studio.
