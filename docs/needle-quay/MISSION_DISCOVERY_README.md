# Needle Quay - Mission Guidance and FX Discovery

Extract the complete ZIP and double-click Launch-NeedleQuay.cmd. It installs local content and opens NeedleQuay-MissionDiscovery.rbxlx in Studio. Press Play. No Roblox uploads or system execution-policy changes. All previous companions, street activities, wardrobe, recognition and voiced reward reactions are included.

## Mission guidance
The mission card shows three visual step segments: completed steps teal, next step amber. Crates, repair controls and record matching show actual server progress. Music objectives show collect / four steps / return. Guidance sends you to Tori or Mica first when the required sound has not been collected, then your Roamer, then the host to check the result. Beat guidance no longer marks an uncollected sound as ready.

The ? button reveals a short contextual hint. After roughly 35 seconds of visible guidance without a step/target change, the hint opens once. Progress resets the hint timer. Guidance never edits your beat, completes a mission or teleports you.

DISCOVER FX tracks the next locked recipe's activity. MAIN MISSION returns to the campaign. Optional FX activities remain discoverable after the main campaign. Signal Repeat guidance first routes through Mica's reply when needed.

## Earned FX recipes
Use Roamer > Sounds > Discovered FX:
- Dock Echo: win Echo Pads; dotted echo with a light room send.
- Vent Room: restore Ivo's vent; a stronger room sound.
- Lantern Space: win Lantern Lock; spacious reverb and light echo.
- Signal Repeat: light the breakwater; a short rhythmic repeat.

Select a pad, APPLY an earned recipe, then AUDITION or play your pattern. Recipes modify only echo, delay time and reverb. Sample trim, pitch, envelope and level remain yours. These are authored recipes for existing processors, not newly introduced DSP effects. Existing Dry, Dotted Echo, Small Room and manual controls remain free. Locked buttons show their unlock requirement. New discoveries receive a short FX banner; loading prior progress does not replay banners.

Availability derives from saved server progress; applying recipes is checked on the server. Resulting pad settings travel with the existing patterns, shelf snapshots and playback paths. Studio persistence remains session-only.

## Existing local content
Select Alissia or Nikolas in Companions. Earn keepsakes from missions and four street games; equip titles, pins, companion charms and Roamer lights in WARDROBE. Nearby residents and companions notice your equipment with short gestures and locally bundled ElevenLabs speech. Voice volume, mute, subtitles and Effects controls remain available. Music-making and activities take priority over ambient reward chatter.
