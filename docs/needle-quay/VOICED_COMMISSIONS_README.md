# Needle Quay — Voiced beat requests

> Historical voice-production and package record. Current commissions use named saved sequences, with **Save Sequence** followed by the host's sequence picker. Old prerecorded Shelf save reminders are suppressed and revised text is shown. The Shelf instructions below are superseded by [MPC v3](MPC_V3_README.md); production provenance and dated verification limits remain historical.

Launch Launch-NeedleQuay.cmd from the extracted folder, then Play in local Studio.
All media stays local. No Roblox uploads or publishing.

Meet Tori, Ivo, Naya or Mica. Opening their conversation plays their beat brief. Use HEAR REQUEST AGAIN to replay it (12-second repeat cooldown). Accept the request, make your beat using its discovered FX, store on Shelf and Save. Return to the host and play the selected saved Shelf entry.

After the excerpt, residents give spoken approval or a specific suggestion: faster, slower, a second audible layer, or four FX hits. An empty saved Shelf selection gets a spoken save reminder. The five visual criteria remain available. Finish all four requests to unlock QUAY PRODUCER.

28 authored lines, seven per resident, produced through ElevenLabs eleven_v4. Tori/Ivo reuse Roger; Naya/Mica reuse Sarah. Eight source takes preserved; first take per resident cut at six measured phrase pauses. Runtime clips are normalized mono PCM16 44.1kHz WAV. Generation IDs, costs, source hashes and cut ranges are preserved in assets/production/needle-quay/commission-voices. Generation cost: 5,244 credits, USD 1.0488.

Speech is positional and stops when you leave the host, enter your studio or start another music activity. Briefs stop when you close the conversation. One resident line at a time; beat excerpts take priority, companion speech is suppressed during resident speech, and ambient audio ducks. Voice volume, mute and subtitles use the existing mix settings. Reactions are selected by server-side saved-pattern rules, not microphone or audio analysis.

Local build and package prepared. No test suite or Studio playtest was run. Voice audibility, listening quality, timing, mobile layout and multiplayer acceptance remain unverified. Loading failures retain subtitles. Audio remains Studio-local. Studio progress lasts only for the current Play session. Nothing committed or pushed.
